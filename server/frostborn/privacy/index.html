Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2024  ·  CYT Bilişim

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No account creation is required to play Frostborn.

We use anonymous crash analytics to improve app stability. These reports contain no personally identifiable information — only device type, OS version, and the technical details of the crash.

In-app purchase records are processed through RevenueCat solely to verify and restore purchases. No payment data passes through our servers.

2. In-App Purchases

All transactions are processed directly by Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat as a purchase verification layer to confirm entitlements and enable purchase restoration across devices.

We do not store, see, or handle your credit card or payment details at any point. For billing disputes, please contact Apple or Google support directly.

3. Advertising

Frostborn may display optional rewarded video advertisements provided by Google AdMob. These ads are always optional — watching them is never required to progress in the game.

Ad providers such as Google may collect device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID) and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can opt out of personalised ads through your device's privacy settings.

4. Children

Frostborn is designed to be suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has inadvertently provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
