Privacy Policy

Frostborn: Last Camp — Last updated: November 8, 2026

Summary: Frostborn: Last Camp collects minimal data necessary to serve ads and manage in-app purchases. We do not require account creation. Your data is never sold to third parties.

Frostborn: Last Camp ("the Game") is developed and published by CYT Bilisim Hizmetleri ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled. By using the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. No Account Required

The Game does not require you to create an account. You can play without providing any personal information such as your name or email address.

2. Data We Collect

2a. Advertising Identifier (AdMob / Google)

We use Google AdMob to show advertisements in the Game. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID (IDFA on iOS / GAID on Android)
  • IP address (for ad targeting and fraud prevention)
  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is used to serve relevant ads and may be used for advertising and analytics purposes. On iOS 14.5+ we request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before accessing the IDFA. You may opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings.

For Google's privacy practices, see: policies.google.com/privacy

2b. Purchase History (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat to manage and verify in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous purchase token issued by the App Store / Google Play
  • Your app user ID (a randomly generated identifier)

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, or payment details. For RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

2c. Crash & Performance Data

The Game may use crash reporting tools to automatically collect crash logs and performance diagnostics to improve stability. This data does not identify you personally.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be shared with:

  • Google AdMob — for advertising (see Section 2a)
  • RevenueCat — for purchase management (see Section 2b)
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — for purchase processing
  • Legal authorities — if required by law

5. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or California, you have additional rights including the right to access, correct, or delete data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@cytbilisim.com.

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time:

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking — toggle off for Frostborn
  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services. Advertising identifiers and anonymous purchase tokens are subject to the retention policies of AdMob and RevenueCat respectively.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance.
