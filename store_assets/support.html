Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Game and tap Settings (gear icon) > Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase. If restore fails, check your internet connection and try again.

How do in-app purchases work?

Frostborn: Last Camp is free to play. Optional purchases include Remove Ads ($1.99), Starter Pack ($2.99), and coin bundles (250, 900, and 2500 coins). Payments are processed securely by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never store your payment information.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. For App Store purchases, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, visit the Google Play order history and tap on the item.

How do I turn off ads?

Purchase the Remove Ads option from the in-game shop. This permanently removes all banner and interstitial ads for a one-time payment.

My progress was lost — what can I do?

Game progress is saved locally on your device. If you uninstall and reinstall the Game, your progress may be reset. We recommend not uninstalling the Game unless necessary. Cloud save features may be added in a future update.

The game is crashing or not loading. What should I do?

Try the following: (1) Close and reopen the Game. (2) Restart your device. (3) Make sure your OS is up to date. (4) Uninstall and reinstall the Game. If the problem persists, contact us with your device model and OS version.

What data does Frostborn collect?

We collect minimal data: an advertising ID for personalised ads (via AdMob) and an anonymous purchase token for purchase management (via RevenueCat). No account creation is required. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Is Frostborn: Last Camp suitable for children?

The Game is rated for ages 7+ (mild cartoon violence — wolves, weather hazards). It contains advertising and in-app purchases. Parental guidance is recommended for younger players.
