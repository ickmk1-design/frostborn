Survive the Freeze

Every decision matters when the world has turned to ice.

🪓

Chop, Build, Survive

Gather wood to fuel your fire and construct new buildings. Every log you chop is one step closer to warmth.

🔥

Keep the Fire Alive

Your campfire is the heart of your camp. Let it die and you lose everything. Manage fuel, build furnaces, and upgrade heating.

🍖

Hunt & Fish for Food

Send survivors to hunt wolves and fish through the ice. Keep your people fed or watch morale and health crumble.

⛺

Build Your Dream Camp

Unlock dozens of buildings: barracks, watchtowers, lumber mills, storage crates, research labs and more.

👥

Rescue & Assign Survivors

Find frozen survivors scattered across the map. Assign them as woodcutters, hunters, builders or guards.

❄

Battle the Blizzard

Random blizzards and enemy raids test your defences. Build perimeter walls, train troops, and prepare for the worst.
